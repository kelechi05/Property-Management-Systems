Our Story

Copen Services Limited was incorporated in January 1992 but started full operations in July 1995. It has an enviable record in the field of construction and engineering, including roads and buildings, erosion control, water resources development, and specialized procurements, where we have completed projects to the satisfaction of our clients. We are duly registered with Federal and State governments, including the Federal Works Registration Board - Federal Ministry of Works and Housing in category D, the highest category, and also with the VAT office.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide honest, professional, and customer-focused land and property services through integrity, transparency, innovation, and excellence. We are committed to helping clients make secure property decisions, building lasting relationships, and contributing to sustainable community development.

Our Vision

To restore trust, confidence, and transparency in the land and property business by delivering reliable real estate solutions that empower individuals, families, and investors to own and manage property with peace of mind.
